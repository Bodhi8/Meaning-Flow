# MeaningFlow — Notebook 01 Guide  
**Semantic Space, Coverage, Opportunity & Authority**

## Purpose

Notebook 01 demonstrates the core MeaningFlow workflow: modeling meaning as a
high-dimensional system, measuring semantic coverage, identifying demand-weighted
opportunities, and diagnosing structural authority flow.

The notebook is designed to be:
- Readable top-to-bottom as a narrative
- Runnable without external data sources
- Interpretable without deep SEO or IR background

It prioritizes **decision support**, not prediction.

---

## What This Notebook Does

The notebook proceeds in four stages:

### Step 3.1 — Semantic Geometry
- Embeds documents, queries, and entities into a shared vector space
- Computes semantic similarity using cosine distance
- Constructs semantic similarity edges
- Measures demand-weighted semantic coverage
- Identifies high-value semantic opportunity gaps

### Step 3.2 — Structural Analysis
- Builds subgraphs for:
  - Internal link authority flow
  - Semantic connectivity
- Computes:
  - Degree metrics
  - PageRank (authority hubs)
  - Betweenness centrality (bottlenecks)
  - Isolates (structural gaps)

### Step 3.3 — Segmented Coverage & Opportunity
- Attributes coverage and opportunity by:
  - Site section
  - Entity domains (via document–entity mentions)
- Enables prioritization by interpretable business segments

### Step 3.4 — Exports & Executive Summary
- Writes CSV outputs to `outputs/exports/`
- Generates a short executive summary memo
- Makes results usable without running the notebook
